# AI use and transparency

**For projects and agreements that involve AI:**

AI use is welcome when its use is transparent, agreed by both sides and appropriately supervised.

Every decision by either party must be accountable to a human. Every form of generative content must be clearly stated as such.

The client is responsible for defining, reviewing and approving any requirements and deliverables. This includes their team members, contractors, and any form of automated/ generative content supplied to Codeconut Ltd.

## Legal notes

Codeconut Ltd. does not apply AI-to-AI automation for any internal communication with clients and members or contractors, like tasks, emails or chats.

It is beyond our scope of responsibility, to take this level of ownership of business decisions, product or project management. Even with best intents, it easily can lead to scope drift, miscommunication, delays and exceeding budgets.

> Codeconut Ltd. is not responsible or liable for harm, additional costs, delays or missed outcomes caused by inaccurate, incomplete, ambiguous or unsupervised AI-generated requests or deliverables supplied by the client.
